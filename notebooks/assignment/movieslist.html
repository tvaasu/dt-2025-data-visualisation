plt.scatter(df['Rating'], 
            df['Box Office (millions)'], 
            s=df['Duration (minutes)'], 
            alpha=0.6, c=df['Year'], cmap='viridis')

plt.title('Box Office vs Rating (Bubble size = Duration, Color = Year)')
plt.xlabel('Rating')
plt.ylabel('Box Office (millions)')
plt.colorbar(label='Year')
plt.show()

This scatter plot explores whether higher-rated movies tend to earn more money. In this dataset, there isn’t a clear linear trend — some movies with average ratings still earned high box office numbers, while some highly rated ones didn’t make as much. Bubble sizes show that longer movies (larger circles) don’t necessarily earn more, but there might be subtle patterns depending on the year.
